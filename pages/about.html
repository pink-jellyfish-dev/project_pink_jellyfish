About Project Pink Jellyfish

Project Pink Jellyfish at its core is a game development studio focused on creating uniqe games for players to experience. However, Project Pink Jellyfish aims to be different from most game studios and attempting projects and types of games that are different, and designed to push boundaries of what we understand can be a game.

To do this, the Pink Jellyfish Project conducts research into the creation and structure of games, publishes theories here and then creates experiments to see if those theories hold in a practical demistration. These experiments can be published here for you to enjoy and experience, and if you would like, share your experience and thoughts on the results.

The studio is run by a single developer, me, who is curently studying computer science. Projects and research from that study may also be pubslished here if they seem to be relevant to the studio's work. I am stil just learning and starting out, and I'd love to have you join me in this journey. And my dream with this project is to eventaully teach game design and development so anyone can create their own dreams and ideas.
