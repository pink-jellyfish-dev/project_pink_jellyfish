An Introduction

Hello! I am an independent game developer set on creating new experiences in games. I view games as a collection of interlocing systems, which can be taken apart and examined individually. These systems can be combined with other systems from different games to create something unique. In doing this we can also learn the relationships between systems and better understand how to use them to make better games.

This site acts as a public journal of my research and findings with this theory of game design. It is also where you can participate in experiments and share your ideas and experiences if you would enjoy that. If any of this interests you at all, fell free to look at some of my recent entries here.
