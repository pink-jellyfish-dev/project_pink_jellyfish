# Project Pink Jellyfish Website 
This website features a steampunk submarine where the crew are all survivors of the robot apocalypse, where they retreated into the sea where it was difficult for the AI to find or follow them. On board there are different characters which the user can talk to, and understand more of the story of why they are there and what they are doing.

These characters are used to explore the nuances of the usage of AI, and one of the characters is an AI bot which many of the characters interact with to show more nuanced views of AI and how it can be used and abused.

## The Submarine (The Pink Jellyfish)

Inside the submarine there are different places that the user can click to explore the different pages, for example in the lab the player can click to see the catalogue of experiments or the most recent experiment will be on a research table or something.

The submarine itself is The Pink Jellyfish, and Project Pink Jellyfish is people running from an AI robot takeover. On board are different things that the crew needs:
- Defenses
- Food Production
- Sleeping Corridors
- Power Production
- Navigation
- Experiment Lab
- Recreation Area
- Storage
- An Observatory
- A Library

### Pages
The needed pages are:
- **Home:** This is the central hub and landing page
    - It shows the whole submarine, or on mobile allows the user to move from room to room.
- **About:** This describes what Project Pink Jellyfish is, and how it works
    - This is talked about by the characters which the person can talk to. Or they can find a link to a page on the top.
- **Journal:** These are the dev logs of projects, and articles exploring things
    - This is in the navigation room as the captains journal
- **Experiments:** This is a list of all the projects and links to where people can play with them
    - This is in the experiment lab room
*Wishful Pages*
- **Chatroom:** A part of the site where people can all chat together and talk about things or share their opinions or ideas.
    - The chatroom is located in the recreation area
- **Archives:** This archives memorable things about the site like chats or favorite posts
    - This is located in the library where the user can paroose the shelfs
- **Fishing Game:** This is a game the users can play where they fish and collect different fish. It would be cool if fish were treated like cards that users could trade.
    - This is located in the observatory, where they can look out the window and use an external fishing rod.
- **Defense Games:** This is where players collectively play games to fight of hoards of enemeis trying to take down the submarine. This is for when we have more traffic to a site and preferrably a server.

As more pages are added, they can be linked to the rooms on the submarine more. The submarine itself can be expanded and upgraded further.

Outside the submarine is the ocean, where the background changes shade depending on the depth of the submarine. It can resurface at different times for different things. Fish on the outside of the submarine change with depth as well, and there are small particles and things that the submarine wanders through. This is all to create the illusion of movement and travel.

### Characters
The characters are people that the crew have collected in their flight from the evil robot. Right now there are only a few characters:
- Captain Jimmy
    - Started The Pink Jellyfish Project
    - Built the submarine
    - Manages most of the operational aspects of the submarine
    - Was working on his dream submarine when attacked by the robots and fled
- Robot Cook Sam
    - Was originally installed by Jimmy to be a cook and assistant and then the robot takeover happend and Sam turned so Jimmy had to shut him down
    - While Jimmy was hiding he reprogrammed Sam to be more autonomous and free, and freed him of the destructive code that was hidden in him
    - Sam loves to cook and is possibly sentient
    - He is so passionate about cooking he will spend hours talking about cooking
    - Sam manages most of the food and housekeeping aspect of the submarine
- Mad Scientist Rick
    - Jimmy found him drifting in the ocean while using a makeshift raft to run from the robots
    - Rick was more than happy to team up with Jimmy
    - Rick manages growing food in the little bioreactor onboard
    - He also manages the experimentation lab
