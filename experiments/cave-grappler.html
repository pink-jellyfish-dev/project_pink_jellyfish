Grappling guns are cool

Cave Grappler

October 26, 2026 · Work in Progress

What could be better than swords and grappling hooks? Less bugs? Maybe. Anyways, this is a game where you have a grappling hook and a sword (duh) and you fight evil enemies while zipping about the map faster than my cat who accidentally drank some of my monster.

If you love fast paced movement based combat games, then this is the game for you. Currently it is just a prototype with only mechanics, but if enough people want I will add more content.
